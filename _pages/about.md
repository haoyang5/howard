I obtained my B.S. degree in Electronic and Communication Engineering from City University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong, in 2022, and the M.Phil. degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, Hong Kong, in 2024. Currently, I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The George Washington University, advised by `Prof. Nan Wu`. My research interests include `FPGA-based accelerator design`, `machine learning`, and `hardware/software co-design` for efficient computing.
